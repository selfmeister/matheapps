# Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 6, 2026

MathPilot and MathMagic are math practice games for children and families. The apps are designed to work without accounts, advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs, purchases, or network services.

## Data Collection

The apps do not collect personal data and do not send personal data to us or to third parties.

The apps do not use:

- User accounts
- Third-party advertising
- Third-party analytics
- App Tracking Transparency tracking
- Location, camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or notification permissions
- In-app purchases or subscriptions

## On-Device Data

The apps save progress and preferences locally on the device using Apple's UserDefaults system. This may include:

- Selected language
- Sound and accessibility preferences
- High score
- Local learning progress
- Local practice history used to show in-app learning suggestions

This information stays on the device. It is not transmitted to us or to third parties.

Users can clear local learning progress inside the app where the reset option is provided. Users can also remove the app from the device to delete its local app data.

## Children's Privacy

The apps are intended to be safe for children. They do not ask children to provide personal information, do not include social features, and do not share data with advertising or analytics providers.

## Changes

If the apps' privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before those changes are released.
